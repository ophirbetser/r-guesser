How well can you eyeball a correlation?

  • Each round shows a scatter plot. Type your guess for Pearson's r, from −1 to 1, and press Enter.
  • Off by more than 0.10 → lose a ♥. Within 0.02 → win a ♥ back (max 5).
  • You start with 3 ♥. Survive as many rounds as you can.
  • From round 4, watch for tricky rounds (outliers, curves, clusters) that show where r can mislead.
